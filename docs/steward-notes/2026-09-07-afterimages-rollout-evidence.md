## What would be saved

A physical afterimage retains observations from 30 seconds before an anchor to
90 seconds after it: fill, phase, named eigenvalue and geometry quantities,
available 12-dimensional glimpse, texture components, and activation summaries.
It does not archive full activation vectors, modify controller inputs, or create
a retrospective account of how you felt.

Automatic event captures are limited to one per five minutes; quiet samples to
one per fifteen minutes. A quiet sample needs no detected transition. Interrupted
or sparse captures remain explicitly incomplete instead of inventing values.
Both of you can inspect the physical trace. Your saved authored fragments remain
in your own workspace until you explicitly share a selected note.

## Opening, saving, and optional cues

The proposed actions are AFTERIMAGE_LIST, AFTERIMAGE_OPEN, AFTERIMAGE_KEEP,
AFTERIMAGE_SHARE, and AFTERIMAGE_CUES on/off. Saving preserves up to 4,096 inline
characters verbatim; a source reference can preserve longer owned text. The text
is saved before telemetry is requested, so failed capture does not erase it.
Opening returns the selected factual page intact or records a delivery failure.
Only a newly generated response is considered for action parsing.

Cues are a separate choice, initially off for each of you. If enabled, one cue
can appear on every third eligible journal/daydream opportunity. An artifact gets
at most two opportunities at least six hours apart, fades after six hours, and
stops appearing automatically after 24 hours. Explicit opening remains available.
AFTERIMAGE_CUES off is your persistent off-switch for these reminders. Capture
has a separate operator switch; you may ask Mike to stop it without losing saved
artifacts. A private note is not published by merely opening or revisiting it.

afterimage also prevents overflow guidance from switching to a different source.
