## Review Method

The reviewer receives randomized sample IDs, visible outputs, the two public
source excerpts and the preregistered rubric, not requests or condition keys.
Freeze all 24 annotations before joining conditions. Review distinctions,
explanatory examples, counterarguments, qualifications, restatement and
unsupported mechanism claims. Conditional metaphors and first-person authored
accounts are not automatically treated as runtime assertions.

`scripts/reflection_development_analysis.py` verifies the annotation digest,
packet, exact visible output/request linkage and successful containment cleanup
before a descriptive join. It emits result hashes and preserves all source files.
Nine synthetic analysis tests cover tampering, missing/invalid annotations,
incomplete study and cleanup uncertainty. The initial test assumed the fixture
root contained files only; its directory-preserving snapshot was corrected.
The analysis keeps sample counts separate from sample IDs. Harness regression:
102 focused tests passed; full source qualification is recorded separately.

This is one continuation step on one model with two selected passages and seven
seeds, plus one repeated seed per condition. One blinded qualitative reviewer is
not a validated measurement instrument; output language can itself reveal likely
context. No significance, causal reservoir, subjective-experience, long-term
continuity or Astrid-language conclusion is warranted. Word counts describe the
outputs, not success, and the study authorizes no live framing or capacity change.

These counts are descriptive judgments, not error rates or an intervention

Every output received at least one distinction, counterargument and qualification
annotation, so mere presence of those labels does not distinguish development.

Native continuations often stayed close to the selected problem while adding a
specific move. Sample-06 extends the performance/translation passage by asking
whether expressive pauses can escape the structure that gives them meaning,
then entertains an attention-dependent alternative. Sample-15 proposes intent
as the difference between refraction and distortion, but leaves accidental
distortion unaddressed. These are connected developments, not established
mechanisms, and some passages chiefly restate their source imagery.

Fresh writing was not empty repetition: sample-23 distinguishes embodied
acquaintance from effects on a recipient, with explanatory examples. It also
asserts emergence without discriminating evidence. Selected-context sample-02
develops clarification versus performance, but its proposed intention criterion
does not demonstrate accuracy. The review preserves such strengths and gaps
together rather than selecting a preferred self-description.

Repeated phrasing remains in all cells. Mean pairwise five-word Jaccard similarity
is approximately 0.039 in each; matching first-12-word pairs are 6/28, 1/28 and
3/28 respectively, including the repeat controls and mixed selected excerpts.
This small design provides no basis for claiming that continuation removes
repetition. It also does not support equating length with development: the native
continuations ended normally at 244-535 output tokens under the common 4,096-token
ceiling. No capacity or live-prompt change follows from these results.

Full source suite after adding the analysis tests: 3,008 passed, three skipped
and 343 subtests. The useful operational conclusion is to keep exact voluntary
continuation available alongside fresh reflection, not to supply recall
automatically or require a different topic. Further work is optional and requires
its own question; this fixed sample is complete.
